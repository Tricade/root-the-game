# Customer enquiries and contract operations

Customers request hosting, archiving, databases or AI inference. Three enquiries are available initially; new enquiries arrive every 15 simulated minutes, with a bounded inbox of twelve. An unanswered enquiry expires after four hours. Declining has no reputation penalty. Request quantities scale with the installed capability of the largest suitable room, including server upgrades.

Accepting is a commitment. The quote checks the selected room's hardware, reserved capacity, existing projects, crew and funds. The setup fee equals thirty minutes of base service revenue. Resources are reserved immediately, and one agent must complete **600 work units** of provisioning before any revenue begins. Agent level, traits and research affect the duration. An assigned agent cannot simultaneously contribute to routine operations, undertake another assignment, change focus, upgrade or retire. A queued setup waits without charging again.

Contracts run for **one, three or seven simulated days**, starting after setup. They retain their own duration, renewal choice, premium commitment and satisfaction. Each room supports up to sixteen named contract batches; a batch can contain many service units. More rooms expand this capacity. Premium changes apply at renewal, preserving current obligations.

## Support and departures

Actual service delivery determines revenue and satisfaction. A short interruption can be repaired before it becomes a complaint. A cumulative deficit equivalent to fifteen minutes of total outage opens a complaint. Two hours can trigger an SLA cancellation notice; customer-care research extends that tolerance by 25%. Partial outages accumulate proportionately. Healthy supply reduces the accumulated deficit over time.

A new SLA notice normally provides up to six hours to repair the underlying service and attempt retention, bounded by the remaining contract term. Support uses 600 agent work units and improves satisfaction, but does not repair hardware or promise renewal. Retention uses 1,200 work units, costs credits and is allowed once per contract term. It succeeds only with adequate supply, with a 65% base chance or 80% after account-management research. Repairing after a cancellation notice does not silently withdraw the notice.

Satisfied customers can also cancel at term end because of budget cuts, consolidation or a competing provider. Commercial reviews occur every six hours, with a deterministic 1.25% departure chance per review. Automatic renewal therefore does not create permanent, guaranteed income. Ended contracts release their own reserved resources; a bounded history records the reason.

Automatic acceptance is **off by default**. Optional acceptance respects capacity, protected cash and a separate hourly setup budget. Automatic assignment is on by default; it assigns available agents to paid work and prioritizes setup. These policies also run offline. Self-maintenance pauses the assigned unit's work, without losing payment or its progress.

The dashboard summarizes contracts, enquiries, service delivery, recurring margin and work waiting for staff. **Customers & contracts** contains sales and support. **Buildings & utilities** contains room profiles, reserves, paid crews, management and central connections. **Automation** contains policies; **Operation plans** contains budgets, deliveries and scheduled work. The physical control-room desks and room console use these same routes and calculations.
